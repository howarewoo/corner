## Three things to keep distinct

| Record           | Purpose                                                 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Routine          | A reusable starting point with exercises and targets.   |
| Planned workout  | A dated session you intend to do.                       |
| Recorded workout | The actual sets, duration, effort, and notes you saved. |

A target is not a completed set. Previous results and editable prefills are starting points, not proof that you performed today's work.

## Plan and log a session

Open **Training → Week**, select the date, and choose **Add workout**. Build a session or use a routine. When you train, open **Start workout** or **Continue logging**, enter actual results, and save or complete the workout.

Follow [plan and record workouts](/training/workouts) for the steps and [reusable routines](/training/routines) for repeated sessions.

## Make room for a changed day

Use **Move / edit** on a planned session when the date or plan changes. Use **Skip** with a reason when the planned session did not happen. A skipped session remains in your history rather than disappearing.

If you completed some work, record that actual work instead of marking completed sets as skipped. Corner asks you to resolve completed sets before skipping a session.

## Look back

Training's **Progress** view includes completed-session totals, actual minutes, completed working sets, effort ratings, lift history, and recovery check-ins. Planned sessions are not counted as completed work. See [profile and progress](/profile-progress).
