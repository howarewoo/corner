---
title: Reusable routines
description: Save a repeatable starting point without rewriting workouts you already planned or performed.
---

## Use a routine as a starting point

A routine stores a reusable session name, kind, duration, exercises, and targets. It is not a dated workout and it contains no record of work performed today.

Open **Add workout** in Training to choose from saved routines or create your own starting point. Review the routine's exercises, set and rep targets, rest, and units before saving or using it. Give it a name that makes sense when you see it in the chooser later.

## Schedule a session

Choose a routine when adding a workout and check the destination date. Review the resulting session before saving: the routine is a starting point, not an automatic commitment that every target is right for that day.

Each dated workout keeps its own snapshot. Editing a reusable routine later does not rewrite workouts you already scheduled or actual results you recorded.

## Adjust the actual day

If today's session needs different targets, edit the dated session. If you want a different starting point for future sessions, update the routine as well. These are different edits with different effects.

Synthetic example: a routine has three sets of a press. You reduce today's planned session to two sets. That dated change does not mean every future use of the routine should have two sets.

A routine never completes sets or records activity automatically. Use [workout logging](/training/workouts#record-the-actual-work) to record the real session.
