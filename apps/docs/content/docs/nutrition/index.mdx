## Choose the right record

| Record       | What it means                                                       |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recipe       | Reusable ingredients, preparation steps, and nutrition per serving. |
| Planned meal | A recipe and number of servings you intend to have on a date.       |
| Food entry   | What you actually ate, with a date, meal slot, and servings eaten.  |
| Grocery list | Ingredients calculated from the selected week's planned portions.   |

Planning food does not record it as eaten. A grocery check does not log a meal. Your food totals come from food entries, not from everything in your planner.

## A simple daily workflow

1. [Save a recipe](/nutrition/recipes) with nutrition per serving.
2. [Add it to your meal plan](/nutrition/meal-planning) for the date and servings you intend.
3. Review that week's groceries and check your pantry.
4. When you eat, [log the meal or add food directly](/nutrition/food-log). Adjust the actual servings if they differ.

You can skip meal planning and log food directly. You can also plan meals before setting targets.

## Use targets as chosen reference values

Daily targets are optional and entered by you in Settings. Corner supports different training-day and recovery-day values. Unset targets are not zero targets or automatic recommendations. See [profile, targets, and progress](/profile-progress).

Nutrition values depend on what you enter. Check package information, quantities, and serving definitions before relying on a total. The synthetic examples in this guide are arithmetic examples, not dietary advice.
